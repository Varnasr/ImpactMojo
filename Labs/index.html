MEL & Evidence
Sampling Design Studio
Walk from your evaluation question to a defensible sampling strategy — simple random, stratified, cluster or multistage — chosen for you with reasons. Sizes every stakeholder group with a rigorous engine (design effect, finite-population correction, power/MDE, non-response buffers), teaches the statistics as you go, and exports a printable sampling plan. Built for South Asian MEL, with a facilitator mode for training.
