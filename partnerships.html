Our partners

Two organisations work with us on the substance of what we publish — not as sponsors of a logo slot, but as the practitioners we check our material against.

Research & MEL Advisory Partner — end to end

Populi

www.populi.co.in ↗

Populi is our end-to-end research partner and our MEL advisory partner. In their own words, they are “a development advisory firm that partners with foundations, donors, and mission-driven organisations to strengthen the effectiveness of social impact initiatives,” specialising in building data and evidence systems that power decision-making, surface what works, and support real-time course correction.

“We meet our partners where they are… Whether it’s a grassroots nonprofit or a large CSR team, Populi builds stage-appropriate, lean M&E systems that they can confidently use.”

That posture is why the partnership works. ImpactMojo teaches MEL to people who mostly do not have a MEL team — a programme officer holding the logframe alongside four other jobs. Populi builds systems for exactly those organisations, so the practice they bring back is sized for the reader we actually have.

What Populi does

  • MEL framework design
  • Theory of Change development
  • Outcome frameworks
  • Monitoring system design
  • Data collection tools
  • Real-time feedback loops
  • Impact evaluations
  • Process evaluations
  • Developmental evaluations
  • Portfolio evaluations
  • Data analysis & visualisation
  • Learning agendas
  • Needs assessments
  • Evidence reviews
  • Grantmaking strategy
  • Fractional M&E Head

What we do together

  • Review of MEL and research material — our courses, labs and toolkits are read against live practice before they go out.
  • Real cases, anonymised — evaluation designs that had to be changed mid-flight are worth more to a learner than a clean textbook example.
  • A route beyond the free material — when a reader needs an actual evaluation or a MEL system built, we point them to Populi rather than pretending a course is a substitute.

Thought Advocacy & Measurement Partner

India Institute

India Institute is our thought advocacy and measurement partner. Evidence that stays inside a report changes nothing; the harder half of this work is turning a finding into an argument that reaches the people who decide, and then measuring whether the argument moved anything at all.

What we do together

  • Thought advocacy — carrying evidence out of the evaluation report and into public argument, in a form policy audiences can act on.
  • Measurement — frameworks for the outcomes that resist counting, including the influence and advocacy work that most logframes quietly leave out.
  • Testing our material against the policy end — our governance, policy and advocacy content is reviewed by people who argue these cases for a living.

How we work with partners

The terms are the same for everyone, and they are deliberately narrow.

  • Editorial independence is not negotiableA partner reviews material, argues with it, and is often right. A partner does not get approval over what we publish, and no partner has ever been given it.
  • Partner-supported content stays freeSupport does not create a paywall. Everything a partnership touches goes out under the same licence as the rest of the platform — CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, open to anyone.
  • Attribution, both waysWhere a partner shaped a piece of material, the page says so. Where we use a partner's published work, it is cited like any other source.
  • No student data, everPartnerships do not come with access to learner records. See our data protection policy for what we hold and why.
  • Written down where it countsMoney in and money out is listed on our transparency page, not buried in an annual PDF.

Ways to partner

Most conversations that reach us fall into one of these.

Research & evaluation

Co-designed studies, evidence reviews and evaluation cases that become teaching material once anonymised.

Curriculum review

Practitioners reading a course, lab or toolkit before publication and telling us where it breaks in the field.

Workshops & teaching

Co-hosted sessions and cohorts. See workshops and dojos for the current formats.

Institutional deployment

Universities and organisations running the material with their own teams, with progress visible to the institution.
