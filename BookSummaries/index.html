What Are Reading Companions?

Reading Companions are interactive, chapter-by-chapter reading guides for essential development texts. They are not summaries — they are designed for practitioners who want to understand, question, and apply the ideas, not just skim them.

  • Chapter-by-chapter navigation with key arguments and evidence
  • Interactive elements to test comprehension and connect ideas
  • Practitioner-focused takeaways for fieldwork and programme design
  • Completely free and open access — no login required

All Reading Companions are part of ImpactMojo's free Specials collection, alongside games, labs, and interactive tools.
