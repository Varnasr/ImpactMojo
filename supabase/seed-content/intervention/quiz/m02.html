Check Your Understanding

Test your grasp of problem diagnosis before moving to theories of change in Module 3.

1 A team can name the intervention it will run before it can state the problem in one falsifiable sentence. This is the clearest sign of: Multiple Choice
2 Households in a district have functioning latrines but many still defecate in the open. Applying behavioural diagnosis, the binding constraint is best classified as: Multiple Choice
3 Adapting Hausmann–Rodrik growth-diagnostics logic to a social problem, the key diagnostic question is: Multiple Choice

Take a problem your team is working on. Classify it as an information, incentive, capacity, or belief/norm problem — then ask whether your current intervention matches that type, or whether you ran the diagnosis backwards from a solution you had already chosen.
