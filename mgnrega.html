The hundred days

MGNREGA is not a scheme that offers work. It is a law that owes it: every rural household that asks is entitled to a hundred days of paid work in a year, and to be paid within a fortnight. This traces what was actually delivered, district by district.

Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 — Schedule II: wages shall be paid within fifteen days of the week in which the work was done, and a delay entitles the worker to compensation.
