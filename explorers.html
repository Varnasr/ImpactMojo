The Data Room

Eight free, interactive explorers built on India's own official surveys. Read on their own, each answers a plain-language question about the country. Read together, they trace one continuous life-course — from a child's first reading test to the wage economy and the ballot box.

  • 8 explorers
  • 7 official surveys
  • State → district where the data allows
  • Every figure validated & source-linked
Free forever · no login · no paywall · the government's own numbers
Why one room? A single survey is a snapshot. Placed side by side, these eight become a story with a spine — the same child who sits in an under-equipped classroom (UDISE+), struggles to read a simple paragraph (ASER), drifts further from the syllabus each year (NAS) and mostly never reaches college (AISHE) grows into the worker counted by PLFS, the migrant traced in the Census, and the voter tallied by the Election Commission. Pick your own state or district and read your own numbers.

How these are built. Every explorer draws on the official published survey — no scraping of unofficial mirrors passed off as source, no invented figures. Where a number is derived (e.g. a district-level average assembled from official learning-outcome figures), the explorer says so plainly and shows how close it lands to the published total. If a figure can't be sourced and validated, it isn't shown.
