ASER Learning Explorer

How many children in rural India can actually read and do basic arithmetic — and how it varies by state, grade and year. Drawn from the Annual Status of Education Report (ASER), India's largest citizen-led survey of children's learning.

Source: ASER 2024, Table 15 · ASER Centre / Pratham
What v1 covers: government-school children, survey years 2018 · 2022 · 2024 (ASER 2024's own comparison window), all 27 major states + All-India, for reading and arithmetic in Std III, V and VIII. Coming next: private & all-school figures, the full skill ladder (letter → word → Std I → Std II text), and earlier survey rounds (2012–2016) — extended the same way from ASER's public reports.

Reading = the child can read a Std II-level "story" comfortably. Arithmetic = Std III & V measure basic subtraction / division as ASER reports them (Std III: at least subtraction; Std V & VIII: division). All figures are for children enrolled in government schools, tested one-on-one in the household. The assessment method is unchanged since 2006, so years are comparable.

Data compiled from the Annual Status of Education Report (Rural) 2024, Table 15 (ASER Centre, facilitated by Pratham).
