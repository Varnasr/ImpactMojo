Assignments that mark themselves

The studios were built so that the thing a student exports is a gradeable artefact. You are marking a LogFrame, not an essay about LogFrames.

  • ToC Studio — exports a causal chain with assumptions made explicit. Mark the assumptions, not the boxes: that is where the thinking is.
  • LogFrame Studio — exports a full matrix. The practice pack on LogFrame building carries a checklist you can hand out as the rubric.
  • Sampling Studio — exports a plan with the arithmetic shown, so you can see whether a student understood power or guessed a number.
  • Impact Evaluation Studio — exports a design with its identifying assumption stated. Mark whether the assumption is plausible for the case, which is the whole skill.
  • Case challenges — ship with rubrics. Designed for groups, and they work as an end-of-term defence.
On plagiarism. Studio exports are built from choices a student makes about a specific programme, so two honest submissions do not look alike. That is a side effect rather than a design goal, but it is a real one.

Assessment and certificates

  • Assess Yourself — every flagship course ends with a six-question auto-graded self-check, with a per-question explanation. Useful as a pre-read gate rather than as summative assessment.
  • Free certificates — learners who complete a course earn a verifiable certificate at no cost. Verified institutional versions are a paid add-on; the learner-facing certificate is not.
  • Lexicons — each flagship has a curated glossary. Worth handing out in week 1; the vocabulary is most of the barrier for students new to the sector.

What is not here

ESG and CSR. ImpactMojo has no dedicated ESG course, and CSR appears only in passing inside a few 101 decks — not as taught material. If your paper covers corporate sustainability reporting, ESG frameworks or CSR compliance under the Companies Act, this kit will not carry that half of it, and we would rather say so than pad the mapping.

Nearest adjacent material, if useful: Climate Essentials 101, the climate evaluation practice pack, and Development Architecture 101 for how the funding system is put together.

No LMS integration. There is no SCORM package and no gradebook sync. Everything is a URL; students open it and you mark what they export.
