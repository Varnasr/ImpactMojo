Short, focused, take-an-artefact-home practice sets for development practitioners. 4 modules. ~3 hours. One concrete deliverable. Sits between blog-post depth and flagship-course commitment.

The Practice Pack contract

Every pack follows the same structure so you know what you're getting before you commit.

① 4 Modules
~25 min each
Short read, one worked example, one exercise prompt, one downloadable template per module.
② ~3 Hours Total
2h reading + 1h exercise
Designed to fit a single afternoon, or four short sittings across a week.
③ One Capstone Artefact
You finish with a deliverable
A ToR. A drafted survey. A logframe. A workshop plan. Something you can actually use Monday morning.
④ Take-Home Pack
Templates + checklists
Everything you build during the pack — exportable and printable. Yours to keep.

Why Practice Packs?

Most practitioner training in India sits at two extremes: a one-hour workshop that's too thin to produce anything, or a multi-week course that asks for commitment most practitioners can't make. Practice Packs are designed for the middle — focused enough to finish in a working day, deep enough to leave you with something usable.

Each pack assumes you are a practitioner with limited time and a real project waiting. The capstone isn't a quiz — it's the thing your programme actually needs.

  • Pairs with existing ImpactMojo content — labs for hands-on tool use, deep dives for evidence, flagship courses for full breadth, blog posts for opinion.
  • Free, no login required — like everything else on ImpactMojo.
  • Living documents — updated as practice evolves and reader feedback arrives.
